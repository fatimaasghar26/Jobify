About Jobify

Jobify is a simple job board built to help students and entry-level job seekers in Pakistan find opportunities in one place.

Instead of checking multiple job sites separately, Jobify collects job listings and shows them in one place. When you click "Apply," you are taken directly to the original job posting.

Where the jobs come from

Job listings are currently collected from Mustakbil.com and Remote OK.
